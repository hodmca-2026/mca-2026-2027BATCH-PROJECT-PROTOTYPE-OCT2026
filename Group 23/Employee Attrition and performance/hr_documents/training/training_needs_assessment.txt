Training Needs Assessment Policy

Purpose
A training needs assessment identifies the knowledge and skills required for a role and compares them with current capabilities. The results help plan relevant learning rather than assigning courses without a clear objective.

Identifying needs
Training needs may be identified through performance discussions, changes in job responsibilities, new systems or processes, quality reviews, employee development goals, or required compliance updates. The assessment should describe the skill gap, its work-related impact, and the expected outcome from training.

Planning
The employee and manager should consider suitable options such as coaching, guided practice, formal courses, peer learning, or certification. The selected activity should match the need, available resources, and timing. Required training should be prioritized according to organizational and role requirements.

Follow-up
After training, the manager and employee should review whether the target skill has improved and whether additional practice is needed. Relevant completion and follow-up records should be maintained in the approved system.

Fairness
Training access should be considered consistently, using role needs and development objectives rather than personal preference.

Questions
Employees should discuss identified skill gaps and learning options with their manager or the training team.
