Parental Leave Policy

Purpose
This policy describes the process for requesting leave related to the birth, adoption, or placement of a child. Eligibility, leave duration, pay status, and supporting documentation depend on applicable law, employment terms, and organizational policy.

Requesting leave
Employees should contact HR as early as reasonably possible when planning parental leave. The employee and HR should review the applicable leave type, anticipated dates, benefit implications, and any required forms. When early notice is not possible, the employee should contact HR as soon as practical.

Planning and return to work
The employee should coordinate work handover and coverage with their manager where appropriate. HR can explain how parental leave interacts with annual leave, benefits, and return-to-work arrangements. Any changes to the expected leave dates should be communicated to HR and the manager.

Responsibilities
Managers should handle requests consistently and protect private employee information. HR should confirm the process and applicable requirements. Employees should submit required information through approved channels.

Questions
Employees should contact HR for confirmation of eligibility and the rules that apply to their circumstances.
