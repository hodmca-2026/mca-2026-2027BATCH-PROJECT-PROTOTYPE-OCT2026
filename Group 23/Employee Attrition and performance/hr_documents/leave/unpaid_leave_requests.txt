Unpaid Leave Request Policy

Purpose
Unpaid leave is time away from work for which salary is not paid, where such leave is permitted by the organization's rules and applicable law. Approval is not automatic and depends on eligibility, the reason for the request, staffing needs, and other relevant requirements.

Request process
Employees should contact their manager and HR before the proposed leave whenever practical. The request should state the requested dates, expected return date, and a general reason if the policy requires one. HR should explain whether a formal application or supporting information is necessary.

Review and decision
Managers should consider coverage, deadlines, the effect on the team, and any relevant policy requirements. HR should check employment terms and explain potential effects on pay, benefits, leave balances, and service-related calculations where applicable. Employees should not assume the request is approved until confirmation is received.

Changes and return
If the dates change, the employee should notify the manager and HR promptly. The return-to-work date should be confirmed in advance where possible.

Questions
HR is the contact for eligibility, documentation, and the applicable approval process.
