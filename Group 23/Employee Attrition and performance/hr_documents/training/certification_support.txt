Professional Certification Support Policy

Purpose
The organization may support employees who pursue job-related certifications, licenses, or professional credentials. Support depends on business relevance, budget, role requirements, and the approval process in effect.

Request process
Employees should discuss the proposed certification with their manager before registering or paying fees. The request should describe the credential, its relevance to current or planned responsibilities, estimated costs, study time, examination requirements, and expected completion date. HR or the training team can identify the required form and approval route.

Funding and reimbursement
Approved support may cover some or all eligible expenses, subject to written approval and the applicable reimbursement rules. Employees should not assume that registration, examination, renewal, travel, or preparation costs will be reimbursed without confirmation. Receipts and proof of completion may be required.

Use of the credential
Employees should provide evidence of completion through the approved channel when requested. Any continuing education or renewal requirement remains subject to the credentialing body's rules and organizational role requirements.

Questions
Employees should contact their manager or the training team for eligibility, budget, and reimbursement details.
