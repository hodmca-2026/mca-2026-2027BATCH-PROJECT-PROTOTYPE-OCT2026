How SHAP is used in this application

Employee Assessment generates SHAP-based feature contributions on demand.

Positive contributions indicate that a feature increased the model's predicted attrition probability; negative contributions indicate that it decreased the predicted probability.

SHAP contributions explain model behavior and should not be interpreted as proof of causal relationships.
