Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) Policy

Purpose
A Performance Improvement Plan (PIP) is a structured process for addressing documented gaps between role expectations and observed work outcomes. A PIP should clarify expectations and provide a fair opportunity to improve; it should not replace regular feedback or required organizational procedures.

Plan contents
The manager should describe the specific work expectations, the evidence supporting the concern, the improvement outcomes required, and how progress will be reviewed. Goals should be understandable and, where practical, measurable and time-bound. The plan should identify support such as coaching, training, clearer priorities, or regular check-ins.

Review and documentation
The manager should discuss the plan with the employee, allow questions, record agreed actions, and schedule progress reviews. Notes should focus on relevant work evidence and agreed next steps. HR may support consistency, explain the process, and advise on applicable procedures.

Outcome
At the review point, the manager should assess progress against the stated expectations and document the outcome. Any next steps must follow organizational policy and applicable law.

Questions
Employees may contact HR to understand the PIP process or raise concerns about unclear expectations.
