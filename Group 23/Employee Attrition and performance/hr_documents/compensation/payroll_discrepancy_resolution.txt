Payroll Discrepancy Resolution Policy

Purpose
This policy describes how employees can report a possible error in salary, deductions, overtime, approved allowances, or other payroll entries. Payroll questions should be handled through an authorized and confidential channel.

Submitting a query
Employees should review the relevant payslip and compare it with approved time records or compensation changes where available. If something appears incorrect, the employee should contact Payroll or HR using the designated process. The query should identify the pay period and the type of discrepancy and include supporting records where appropriate. Sensitive financial information should not be posted in public or shared team channels.

Review and correction
Payroll or HR should check the relevant source records and explain whether a correction is needed. If more information is required, the employee should receive instructions on the next step. Any adjustment should be processed under the organization's payroll controls and applicable requirements.

Tracking
Employees should retain the query reference or written confirmation until the issue is resolved. Managers should approve only the entries they are responsible for and should not alter payroll records outside authorized procedures.

Questions
Employees should contact the payroll team or HR to find the approved reporting channel and status of a query.
