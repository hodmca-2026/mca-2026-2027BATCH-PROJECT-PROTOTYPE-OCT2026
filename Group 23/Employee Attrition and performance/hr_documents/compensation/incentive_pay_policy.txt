Incentive Pay Policy

Purpose
Incentive pay may be used for eligible roles or programs to recognize defined business results, sales outcomes, project milestones, or other approved performance measures. The existence of an incentive program does not guarantee an award.

Plan rules
The applicable written plan should describe eligible roles, measurement period, performance measures, calculation method, approval authority, payment timing, and any conditions or exclusions. Employees should review the rules that apply to their position and ask questions before relying on a projected payment.

Measurement and approval
Performance or business results should be calculated using approved records and the plan's stated method. Exceptions, adjustments, and disputes should be handled by the designated plan owner and approval authority. Managers should not promise amounts or change plan terms outside their authority.

Payment and changes
Approved incentive payments are processed through the organization's compensation and payroll procedures and may be subject to applicable deductions or rules. Plan changes should be communicated through official channels and applied according to the plan and applicable law.

Questions
Employees should contact HR, Payroll, or the named plan administrator for current eligibility, calculation, and payment details.
