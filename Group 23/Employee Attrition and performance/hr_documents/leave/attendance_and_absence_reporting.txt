Attendance and Absence Reporting Policy

Purpose
This policy explains how employees should report attendance issues, late arrival, missed shifts, and unplanned absence. Employees should follow their team's approved scheduling and timekeeping procedures.

Reporting an absence
If an employee cannot attend work or expects to arrive late, they should notify their manager using the designated communication channel as soon as reasonably possible. The notice should provide an expected duration or next update when that information is available. In an emergency, the employee should contact the manager when it is safe and practical to do so.

Recording time
Employees should record working time, breaks, and absence accurately in the approved timekeeping or HR system. Corrections should be requested through the established process and should include enough information for the record to be checked.

Manager and HR responsibilities
Managers should review attendance records consistently, address discrepancies privately, and refer recurring or unclear issues to HR. Attendance data should not be altered to hide an absence or create an inaccurate record.

Questions
Employees should ask their manager or HR which reporting channel and timekeeping procedure applies to their role.
