Flexible Work Arrangements Policy

Purpose
Flexible work arrangements may include adjusted start and finish times, hybrid work, remote work, compressed schedules, or other approved working patterns. Availability depends on job duties, business requirements, employment terms, and organizational rules.

Request and review
Employees should discuss a proposed arrangement with their manager and follow the formal request process published by HR. The request should describe the proposed schedule, expected duration, coverage considerations, and how communication and work outcomes will be maintained. Managers should consider the role requirements, team coordination, service coverage, security, and fairness across comparable requests.

Approval and review
An arrangement should not be treated as approved until the designated approver confirms it. Approved arrangements may be reviewed if role duties, team needs, performance expectations, or operational conditions change. Any agreed changes should be documented.

Responsibilities
Employees remain responsible for meeting role expectations and following information-security, attendance, and timekeeping rules. Managers should communicate measurable expectations and review outcomes consistently.

Questions
Employees should contact HR for the formal request route and any role-specific restrictions.
