Employee Wellbeing Program Policy

Purpose
The employee wellbeing program promotes healthy work practices and helps employees find information about available wellbeing resources. Program availability can vary by location, employment terms, and organizational arrangements.

Possible resources
Depending on the program, resources may include wellbeing workshops, stress-management information, physical activity initiatives, ergonomic guidance, work-life balance resources, or signposting to professional support. A resource should not be treated as a substitute for medical advice or emergency services.

Participation
Employees should use the enrollment or registration process communicated by HR. Participation rules, schedules, and any costs or eligibility conditions should be checked before joining a program. Employees may ask HR about accessibility or reasonable adjustments needed to participate.

Privacy
Employees should not be required to disclose private health details to their manager merely to ask about general wellbeing resources. Any personal information collected by a provider should be handled under applicable privacy rules and the organization's procedures.

Responsibilities
HR should publish current program information and explain how to access resources. Managers should support reasonable participation while planning team coverage.

Questions
Employees should contact HR for the current program list and access instructions.
