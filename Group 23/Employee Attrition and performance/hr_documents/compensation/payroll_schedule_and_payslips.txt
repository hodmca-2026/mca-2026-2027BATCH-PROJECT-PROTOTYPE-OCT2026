Payroll Schedule and Payslip Access Policy

Purpose
This policy explains how employees can find payroll dates and access their payslips. Pay frequency, cut-off dates, payment methods, and payslip availability are determined by the organization's payroll calendar and applicable requirements.

Payroll calendar
The current payroll calendar should be published through an official employee channel. Changes to scheduled processing dates should be communicated by Payroll or HR. Employees should submit approved time, overtime, expense, and compensation changes by the applicable cut-off so they can be considered for the relevant pay period.

Payslip access
Employees should access payslips through the authorized payroll or HR system and protect their login credentials. Payslips may contain confidential personal and financial information and should not be shared except through approved channels or where disclosure is required.

Corrections and access problems
Employees who cannot access a payslip or notice a possible discrepancy should contact the payroll support channel. They should identify the pay period and describe the problem without sending passwords or other authentication secrets.

Responsibilities
Payroll should maintain the official calendar and access instructions. Employees and managers should submit accurate information within published cut-offs.

Questions
Employees should use the official Payroll or HR contact for schedule, payslip, or access questions.
