Mandatory Compliance Training Policy

Purpose
Mandatory compliance training communicates rules employees must understand to carry out their work responsibly. Assigned topics may include information security, data privacy, workplace conduct, anti-harassment, health and safety, records handling, or other requirements relevant to the role.

Assignment and completion
Employees should review training assignments in the organization's designated learning system and complete them by the deadline communicated with the assignment. Some roles may require additional role-specific modules. Employees who cannot access the course or need an accommodation should contact the assigned support team before the deadline where practical.

Evidence of completion
Completion records or assessment results may be stored in the learning management system. Employees should complete the assigned course themselves and follow the course rules. A manager should not mark training complete unless the approved system or administrator confirms completion.

Overdue training
Managers may receive completion reports and should remind employees about outstanding requirements. Questions about extensions or applicability should be directed to HR, Compliance, or the designated training owner rather than ignored.

Responsibilities
Employees are responsible for completing assigned training and asking for clarification when a requirement is unclear.

Questions
Employees should use the contact shown in the course assignment or ask HR for routing help.
