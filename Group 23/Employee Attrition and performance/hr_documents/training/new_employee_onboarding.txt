New Employee Onboarding Policy

Purpose
Onboarding helps new employees understand their role, workplace practices, systems, team contacts, and organizational expectations. The onboarding plan should be appropriate to the employee's role and work location.

Before and during the first days
HR or the designated onboarding coordinator should provide required joining information, explain administrative tasks, and direct the employee to approved policies and systems. The manager should discuss role responsibilities, initial priorities, team processes, communication practices, and where to ask for help.

Training and access
Required system access should be requested through approved procedures. The employee should complete assigned orientation and mandatory training within the timeframes communicated by the organization. Access should be limited to what the role requires.

Progress check-ins
The manager should arrange check-ins during the onboarding period to answer questions, review early objectives, and identify support or training needs. Completion of onboarding activities should be recorded where required.

Responsibilities
Employees should review the information provided, complete assigned tasks, and report missing access or unclear instructions. HR and managers should coordinate so that essential steps are not missed.

Questions
New employees should contact their manager, onboarding coordinator, or HR for assistance.
