Bereavement Leave Policy

Purpose
Bereavement leave provides time away from work following the death of a family member or another person covered by organizational policy. Eligibility, permitted duration, paid or unpaid status, and any documentation requirements are determined by the organization's rules and applicable law.

Notification and request
Employees should notify their manager as soon as reasonably possible that they need time away. If the employee cannot complete the formal request immediately, the manager or HR should explain how to record the absence afterward. HR may clarify which relationships or circumstances are covered by the policy.

Privacy and support
Managers should respond with sensitivity, avoid requesting unnecessary personal details, and share information only with people who need it to process the absence. Employees may ask HR about available employee assistance or wellbeing resources.

Recording the absence
The employee should submit the required leave entry through the HR system when practical. HR or the manager should ensure the absence is recorded under the correct leave category rather than assumed to be annual or sick leave.

Questions
Employees should contact HR to confirm eligibility, required records, and any support available.
