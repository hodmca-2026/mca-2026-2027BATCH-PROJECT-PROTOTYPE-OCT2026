Business Expense Reimbursement Policy

Purpose
The organization may reimburse reasonable business expenses that employees incur while performing approved work activities. Eligibility, spending limits, required approvals, and non-reimbursable items are defined by the current expense policy.

Before spending
Employees should obtain required approval before making a purchase or booking travel. They should use approved suppliers, purchasing tools, or payment methods when required. Personal purchases should not be represented as business expenses.

Submitting a claim
Claims should be submitted through the designated expense system within the stated submission period. Each claim should describe the business purpose, date, amount, currency, and relevant cost category. Itemized receipts and approval records should be attached where required. Missing documentation should be explained and handled according to the expense process.

Review and payment
The designated reviewer should check business purpose, policy compliance, supporting evidence, and approval before authorizing reimbursement. Employees should respond promptly to requests for clarification and should not submit the same expense more than once.

Questions
Employees should contact Finance or HR for current limits, claim deadlines, eligible categories, and exception procedures.
